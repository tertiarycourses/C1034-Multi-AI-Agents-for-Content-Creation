# Persona template (Lab 6)

For each persona:
- **Name and one-line summary** (a label, not a real person)
- **Who** — age band, life stage, share of respondents (n = ?)
- **Top worries** — with counts from the data
- **Where they look** — channels and formats, with counts
- **What makes them trust us** — with counts
- **Checklist gaps** — the items they most often leave unticked
- **What they say** — 2 short quotes from enquiries.csv, verbatim, with ids
- **Confidence** — HIGH (n >= 30), MEDIUM (20-29), LOW (< 20)
- **INFERENCE** — anything you concluded that the data does not show directly,
  labelled as such
