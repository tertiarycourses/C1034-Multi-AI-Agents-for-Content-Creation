# LinkedIn post
1. Line 1 stands alone — readers see two lines before "…more".
2. 120-250 words; one idea; paragraphs of one or two sentences.
3. One proof point from data/facts-2026.md, with its source.
4. End with a question or one call to action and the UTM link
   (?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=<campaign>).
5. Three relevant hashtags; tag no one without their consent.
6. Short disclaimer last; save with the frontmatter in
   data/publishing-spec.md.
7. One image from the Creative Designer (1200 x 627 PNG beside the
   post) with the image: and alt: lines filled in.
