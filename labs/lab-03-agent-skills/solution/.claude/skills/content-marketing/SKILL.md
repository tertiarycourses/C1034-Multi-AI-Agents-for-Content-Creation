# Content marketing
1. Three pillars from data/business-brief.md (e.g. retirement, CPF and
   SRS, family money). Every idea belongs to one pillar.
2. Tag each idea with its funnel stage: aware (reach), consider
   (lead magnet, newsletter) or book (a free chat).
3. One call to action per stage: follow, download the checklist, book.
4. Repurpose one idea: blog article -> LinkedIn and Facebook posts ->
   newsletter tip -> 60-second video. Same claims everywhere.
5. Measure each stage: reach, downloads, chats booked.
