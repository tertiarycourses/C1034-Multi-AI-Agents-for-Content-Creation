# Blog post
1. One search intent and one keyword phrase; research from research/
   or the web, and cite every source with its date.
2. Title under 60 characters with the keyword; meta description under
   155 characters.
3. 700-1,000 words: an intro that answers the question, H2 sections,
   a short FAQ (three questions), a summary.
4. Every figure from data/facts-2026.md; no fees, no promised returns.
5. One call to action (the checklist or a free chat) and the full
   disclaimer. Save content/blog/<slug>.md with publishing frontmatter.
