# Lead magnet
1. The reader's problem in one line, and one promise ("Know where you
   stand in 10 minutes").
2. Format that fits the promise: checklist, calculator or short guide.
3. Content from data/checklist-items.md only; every figure from the
   facts sheet.
4. Opt-in page: what they get, a form with a REQUIRED PDPA consent box
   and a SEPARATE unticked newsletter opt-in.
5. The delivery email (a draft) and the next step: book a free chat.
6. Measure: visits -> downloads -> chats booked.
