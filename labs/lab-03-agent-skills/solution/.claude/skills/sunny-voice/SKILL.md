# Sunny voice
1. Warm, calm, plain English — a friend who is good with money.
2. Singapore context (CPF, HDB, SRS); explain each acronym once.
3. No sentence over 25 words. One idea per paragraph. One next step.
4. Banned: guaranteed, risk-free, get rich, secret, hack, skyrocket,
   can't lose, best returns, act now.
5. Social posts end: "Sunny says: one small step today."
