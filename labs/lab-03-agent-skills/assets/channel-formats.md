# Channel formats (Lab 3)

## LinkedIn post
- 120-250 words. A first line that works on its own (it is all most people
  see). Short paragraphs. One idea. 3 hashtags at the end. Link to the site
  with UTM tags. Short disclaimer as the last line.

## Facebook post
- 60-150 words, warmer and more personal. A question to invite comments.
  Link with UTM tags. Short disclaimer. Suggest one image (alt text too).

## Newsletter issue (the Sunny Sunday email)
- Subject (under 50 characters) and preview text (under 90).
- One tip, one calculator or checklist nudge, one call to action.
- 250-400 words. Unsubscribe link, the firm's address and the short
  disclaimer in the footer.

## YouTube explainer
- 60-90 seconds, 5-7 scenes. Each scene: seconds, words on screen (under 8
  words), voice-over.
- Title under 60 characters; description with chapters, the site link with
  UTM tags and the short disclaimer; 5-8 tags.

## Links and UTM tags
Every link goes to our own website, http://localhost:8080/ (start it with
"start my website"), never to a private share link or the public site:
http://localhost:8080/?utm_source=<linkedin|facebook|newsletter|youtube>&utm_medium=<social|email|video>&utm_campaign=know-your-number
