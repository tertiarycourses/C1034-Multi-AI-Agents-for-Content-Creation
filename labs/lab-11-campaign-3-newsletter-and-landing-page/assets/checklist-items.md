# The 10-minute Money Check-up Checklist

1. I have 3 to 6 months of expenses saved as an emergency fund.
2. I know how much I spend each month, roughly.
3. My high-interest debts (credit cards, personal loans) are paid off or have a plan.
4. I have a hospitalisation plan and I know what it covers.
5. My life and critical illness cover would look after my family.
6. I've made my CPF nominations.
7. I have a will and a Lasting Power of Attorney.
8. I've considered SRS contributions to lower my income tax.
9. My investments match my goals and I've reviewed them this year.
10. I know my retirement number and I'm on track for it.
