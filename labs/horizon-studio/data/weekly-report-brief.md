# The weekly growth report (Lab 13)

Owner: Growth Analyst. Skill: campaign-report. Every Monday at 8am, as a
Gmail DRAFT to Rachel — never sent.

1. **Last week in one line.**
2. **By channel** — a table: clicks, checklist downloads, chats booked,
   spend (S$), cost per chat.
3. **What worked, what did not** — three bullets.
4. **Next month's S$4,000** — the split, one reason per channel.
5. **One decision for Rachel** — a single line.

Subject: "Horizon weekly growth report — <date>". No personal data.
