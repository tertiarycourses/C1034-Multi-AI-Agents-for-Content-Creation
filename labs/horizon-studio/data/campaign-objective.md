# Campaign objective — "Know Your Number" (Lab 5)

**Period:** November 2026 to January 2027.
**Business objective:** 40 booked free chats a month by March 2027.
**Primary action:** download the Money Check-up Checklist -> book a free chat.
**Core message:** You can know your retirement number in 30 minutes, in plain
English, without being sold anything.
**Audiences:** young professionals (25-34), mid-career families (35-49),
pre-retirees (50-62).
**Proof we can use:** facts-2026.md (CPF retirement sums, SRS cap, reliefs),
the calculator (as an illustration), the six testimonials word for word,
"free 30-minute chat", "one-page plan within two weeks".
**Never:** returns, fee amounts, fear, invented stories presented as real.
