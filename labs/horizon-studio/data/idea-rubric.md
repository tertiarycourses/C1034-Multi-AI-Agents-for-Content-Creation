# Idea scoring rubric (Lab 5) — score each 1-5, then weight

| Criterion | Weight | 5 means |
|---|---|---|
| Audience fit | 30% | Answers a top worry of the target persona |
| Evidence available | 20% | Every claim can be backed by facts-2026.md or the site |
| Path to a chat | 20% | Leads naturally to the checklist or a free chat |
| Effort | 15% | The team can make it in under 3 hours |
| Compliance risk | 15% | No projection, return or testimonial risk (5 = lowest risk) |

Weighted score out of 100 = sum(score x weight x 20). Shortlist ideas at
70+. Keep every idea's author. Ties: prefer the one with stronger evidence.
