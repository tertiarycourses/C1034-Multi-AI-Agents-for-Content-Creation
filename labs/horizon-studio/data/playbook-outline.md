# Content strategy playbook — outline (Lab 14)

1. **Goal and measures** — 40 chats a month by March 2027; cost per
   chat; checklist downloads; defects caught before a person.
2. **Who we serve** — the personas (Lab 6), with confidence.
3. **What we publish** — themes, channels, formats, cadence (Labs 5-7).
4. **The agent roster** — every agent and scheduled task: job, tool
   (Claude or Claude Code), model, tools it may use, what it may never do.
5. **The workflow** — research -> write -> review -> human approval ->
   publish, per channel, with the hand-offs.
6. **Approval matrix** — who approves what, by risk (e.g. anything with a
   CPF or tax figure: a planner AND Rachel).
7. **Responsible AI rules** — responsible-ai-checklist.md.
8. **When something goes wrong** — pull the post, correct, log, tell the
   people affected; pause the scheduled jobs.
9. **Review** — a monthly numbers review; a quarterly review of the facts
   sheet, the skills and the agents.
