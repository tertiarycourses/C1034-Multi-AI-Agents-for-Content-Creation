# Horizon Wealth Planning — the founder's brief

From Rachel Goh, founder and principal planner. October 2026.

We have helped 1,200+ Singapore families since 2010. Almost all new clients
still come from referrals — and referrals are slowing. Younger people look
for money help on LinkedIn, Facebook, YouTube and in their inbox, and we are
invisible there.

**My goal:** 40 booked free chats a month by March 2027 (we book
about 18 today), without hiring a marketing team.

**What I have:** three planners (Rachel Goh, Marcus Lee, Nur Aisyah), a part-time
marketing executive (Jun Wei, about 12 hours a week), a marketing budget
of S$4,000 a month, our website and our six services
(services.csv). The last 12 months are in firm-metrics.csv.

**What I need to know before I spend a dollar:**
1. Who in Singapore looks for independent financial planning, on which
   channels, and why now?
2. What does our funnel say — how many more visitors and enquiries do 40
   chats a month need?
3. Who are we up against — planning firms, robo-advisers, bank advisers,
   finfluencers — and what content do they publish?
4. What should we do first: which people, which channels, which themes?

**My rules:** no hard sell, no jargon, nothing that sounds like a guaranteed
return. We are regulated, and our reputation is everything.
