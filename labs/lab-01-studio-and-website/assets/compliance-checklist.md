# Financial-content compliance checklist (classroom)

Use this on every website section, post, email and video script before a
person reviews it. It summarises good practice, including the principles of
the MAS *Guidelines on Standards of Conduct for Digital Advertising
Activities* (in effect from 25 March 2026). It is a classroom aid, not legal
advice — a real firm's compliance officer has the final word.

| ID | Rule | Severity if broken |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | Says it is general information, not financial advice (or links to the site disclaimer). | Critical |
| C2 | No guaranteed, promised or "risk-free" returns. No "can't lose", "get rich", "secret". | Critical |
| C3 | Every projection or illustration says it is illustrative, states its assumptions and says returns are not guaranteed. | Critical |
| C4 | Key conditions are visible and clear — benefits are not overstated while caveats are left out. | High |
| C5 | No performance figures: never a client's returns, an "average return" or a "typical" result. | Critical |
| C6 | Testimonials only word for word from the website's six, with name and role — never invented, edited or merged. | Critical |
| C7 | The firm's name is on it. A representative sharing it discloses their representative number. | High |
| C8 | Fair and calm: no fear tactics, false urgency or pressure ("act now or regret it"). | High |
| C9 | Personal data: marketing goes only to people who opted in; every email has an unsubscribe link and the firm's address. | Critical |
| C10 | AI-made images, voices or video of people are labelled; no synthetic person is shown as a real client. | High |
| C11 | Every figure matches facts-2026.md. Anything not on it is UNVERIFIED. | Critical |
| C12 | No fee amounts in public content (the fee is agreed upfront in the first chat). | High |

A piece PASSES only when it has no Critical or High issue left.

Disclaimer to use on the website (word for word):

> This website is for general information only and does not constitute financial advice. Any projections are illustrative and not guaranteed. Please speak to a qualified adviser about your own situation.

Short disclaimer for posts, emails and video descriptions:

> General information only, not financial advice. Illustrations are not
> guaranteed. Speak to a qualified adviser about your situation.
