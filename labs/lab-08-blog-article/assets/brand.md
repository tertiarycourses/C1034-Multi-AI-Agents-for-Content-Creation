# Horizon brand notes

**Name:** Horizon Wealth Planning (short: Horizon). **Mascot:** Sunny, a smiling cartoon sun.
**Tagline:** Friendly financial planning for sunny days and rainy ones

## Voice — "Sunny"
- Plain English, warm and calm, like a friend who is good with money.
- Singapore context: CPF, HDB, kopi, SRS. Explain any term the first time
  (e.g. "SRS (Supplementary Retirement Scheme)").
- No jargon, no hard sell, no fear. One clear next step.
- Banned words: guaranteed, risk-free, get rich, secret, hack, skyrocket,
  can't lose, best returns, act now.
- Sign-off on social posts: "Sunny says: one small step today."
