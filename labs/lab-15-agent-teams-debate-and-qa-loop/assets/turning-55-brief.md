# The Turning 55 campaign (Lab 15)

Owner: the Marketing Team Lead, run as an agent team in Claude Code.
Window: January 2027. Budget: S$1,200 of the month's S$4,000.

## The strategy on the table
"Lead January with 'Turning 55? What happens to your CPF' — a plain-English
explainer of the retirement sums and CPF LIFE for pre-retirees — on
YouTube and in the Sunny Sunday email, inviting them to a free 30-minute
chat."

## Part 1 — argue it before you run it
- An ADVOCATE argues for the strategy; a CRITIC argues against it.
- Every point cites data/campaign-results.csv, strategy/personas.md,
  data/facts-2026.md or data/compliance-checklist.md — no opinions
  without evidence.
- They message each other directly: two rounds of rebuttal at most.
- The Lead writes strategy/turning-55-decision.md: pros, cons, a verdict
  (go / go with conditions / no-go) and the conditions. A person decides.

## Part 2 — the QA loop
- Start from data/turning-55-draft.md (an intern's first draft).
- Deliverables in content/turning-55/: email.md, video-script.md and one
  thumbnail image with alt text.
- The QA Auditor audits every piece with fact-check and fin-compliance and
  writes review/turning-55-qa.md — one section per round, PASS or FAIL per
  check, with the line to fix.
- FAIL goes straight back to the writer, never through the Lead. Three
  rounds at most; after that the Lead escalates to a person.
- PASS goes to the designer, then the Lead submits for approval.

## Rules
- Figures only from data/facts-2026.md, named as 2026 figures.
- General information, not advice. No promised payouts or returns; no
  "everyone should".
- The website disclaimer on the email, word for word.
