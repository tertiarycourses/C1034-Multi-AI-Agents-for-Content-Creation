# Lab 6 Team Contract

Writer
- input: supplied source-labelled evidence
- output: concise cited draft
- authority: no approval authority and no write tools

Reviewer
- input: draft plus shared evidence
- output: support and citation finding
- authority: emit the exact complete message APPROVED only when the draft is grounded

Termination
- semantic completion: reviewer emits the exact complete message APPROVED
- hard boundary: maximum six messages
- interpretation: a hard-limit stop is incomplete, not approved
